To find what percentage one number is of another, divide the part by the total and multiply by 100.

```txt
part / total x 100
```

For example, if you want to know what percentage 45 is of 200:

```txt
45 / 200 x 100 = 22.5%
```

That means 45 is 22.5% of 200.

## Identify the part and the total

The key is choosing the right base:

- The part is the amount you are comparing.
- The total is the full amount or base.

In the question "what percentage is X of Y?", X is usually the part and Y is the total. If you switch them, the result changes.

## Common examples

| Question                       |     Calculation | Result |
| ------------------------------ | --------------: | -----: |
| What percentage is 18 of 60?   |   18 / 60 x 100 |    30% |
| What percentage is 75 of 300?  |  75 / 300 x 100 |    25% |
| What percentage is 120 of 500? | 120 / 500 x 100 |    24% |
| What percentage is 7 of 10?    |    7 / 10 x 100 |    70% |

If you want the broader logic behind this formula, read [how to calculate percentages](/en/blog/math/how-to-calculate-percentages).

## Where this is useful

In shopping, this calculation can show how much of a budget you used. If you spent $250 out of a $1,000 budget, you used 25%.

In grades, the part can be correct answers and the total can be all questions. If you got 7 out of 10 correct, your score is 70%.

In goals, the part can be current progress and the total can be the target. If you have completed 120 out of 500 units, your progress is 24%.

## Common mistakes

The most common mistake is reversing part and total. Calculating 45 out of 200 is not the same as calculating 200 out of 45. The second result is greater than 100%.

Another issue is using a total of zero. You cannot divide by zero, so the percentage is not defined when there is no base for comparison.
