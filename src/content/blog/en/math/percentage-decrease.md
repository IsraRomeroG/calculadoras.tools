## Formula

```txt
percentage decrease = (original - new) / original x 100
```

If a price falls from 250 to 200:

```txt
(250 - 200) / 250 x 100 = 20%
```

The decrease is 50 units, or 20% of the original value.

The original amount matters. A drop from 100 to 80 is 20%, while a rise from 80 back to 100 is 25%. The same 20-unit difference uses a different baseline in each direction.

A discount applies a chosen percentage to a price. Percentage decrease measures the actual change between two known values. If you already know the percentage to remove, see [how to subtract a percentage](/en/blog/math/subtract-a-percentage).
