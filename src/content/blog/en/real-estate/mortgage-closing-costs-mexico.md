Buying a home costs more than the down payment and mortgage. In Mexico, initial expenses may include notary work, deed formalization, public-registry charges, acquisition taxes or duties, appraisal, lender investigation, origination fees and insurance.

The amount varies by state, property value, lender and transaction. Some costs are paid upfront; others may be financed and then generate interest. Ask for an itemized estimate rather than applying one universal percentage.

Keep three budgets separate: buyer cash for the down payment, transaction and financing costs, and the recurring all-in housing payment. The [mortgage calculator](/en/real-estate/mortgage-calculator) models principal and interest; use the [closing costs calculator](/en/real-estate/closing-costs-calculator) for an initial estimate and confirm with the notary and lender.

[CONDUSEF](https://www.condusef.gob.mx/?idc=848&idcat=1&p=contenido) lists the down payment, appraisal, notary costs, insurance and fees among the items buyers should review.
