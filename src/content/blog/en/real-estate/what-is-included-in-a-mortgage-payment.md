The base mortgage payment covers principal and interest. A real lender payment may also collect life or property insurance, taxes, servicing charges or other contract-specific amounts.

If the base payment is `$17,994.52`, monthly insurance is `$900` and another charge is `$150`:

```txt
all-in payment = 17994.52 + 900 + 150 = 19044.52
```

Purchase expenses such as the down payment, appraisal, notary work, registration and taxes are normally separate from the recurring principal-and-interest calculation, although some costs may be financed.

Read the lender’s quotation line by line: principal, rate, term, base payment, every recurring charge, initial cash required, total repayment and Mexico’s CAT where applicable.
