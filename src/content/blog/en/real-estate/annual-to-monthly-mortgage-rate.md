For a nominal annual rate with monthly payments:

```txt
monthly rate = annual rate / 12
9% / 12 = 0.75% = 0.0075
```

On an opening balance of `$2,000,000`, first-month interest is `$2,000,000 x 0.0075 = $15,000`. From a payment of `$17,994.52`, the remaining `$2,994.52` reduces principal.

Do not enter 9% as a monthly rate or use Mexico’s CAT as though it were the contractual interest rate. CAT is a broader cost indicator. Effective annual rates also require a different conversion:

```txt
monthly effective rate = (1 + annual effective rate)^(1/12) - 1
```

Keep sufficient decimals and round the displayed payment only at the end. The [mortgage calculator](/en/real-estate/mortgage-calculator) expects an annual rate.
