An amortization schedule separates each payment into principal and interest and shows the remaining balance.

For `$2,000,000` at 9% over 20 years:

| Month | Payment | Principal | Interest | Balance |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | $17,994.52 | $2,994.52 | $15,000.00 | $1,997,005.48 |
| 2 | $17,994.52 | $3,016.98 | $14,977.54 | $1,993,988.50 |
| 3 | $17,994.52 | $3,039.61 | $14,954.91 | $1,990,948.90 |

First-month interest is `$2,000,000 x 0.0075 = $15,000`. As principal falls, interest falls and more of the level payment reduces the balance.

Use a schedule to compare terms, verify extra payments and identify total interest. Insurance and other charges belong in separate columns. Generate and export the complete schedule with the [mortgage calculator](/en/real-estate/mortgage-calculator).
