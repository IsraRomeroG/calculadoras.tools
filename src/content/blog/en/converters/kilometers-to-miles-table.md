## Race distance conversions

Popular race distances are often expressed in kilometers, while some apps, training plans and events use miles. These are the equivalents:

| Race | Distance in kilometers | Approximate miles |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 5K | 5 km | 3.11 mi |
| 10K | 10 km | 6.21 mi |
| Half marathon | 21.0975 km | 13.11 mi |
| Marathon | 42.195 km | 26.22 mi |

[World Athletics](https://worldathletics.org/disciplines/road-running/marathon) sets the marathon distance at `42.195 km`, equivalent to 26 miles and 385 yards. A half marathon is exactly half that distance: `21.0975 km`.

These figures convert distance, not pace. The guide to [pace per kilometer and pace per mile](/en/blog/converters/pace-per-kilometer-vs-mile) explains the difference.

These equivalents help interpret a route, but they do not convert speed limits. A distance of `50 mi` equals `80.47 km`; a speed of `50 mph` is a different quantity and must be written as `80.47 km/h`.

## How these values are calculated

[NIST](https://www.nist.gov/pml/special-publication-811/nist-guide-si-appendix-b-conversion-factors/nist-guide-si-appendix-b9) publishes the factor:

```txt
1 mi = 1.609344 km
```

To convert kilometers to miles:

```txt
miles = kilometers / 1.609344
```

To convert miles to kilometers:

```txt
kilometers = miles × 1.609344
```

For example:

```txt
30 km / 1.609344 = 18.641135... mi ≈ 18.64 mi
30 mi × 1.609344 = 48.28032 km ≈ 48.28 km
```

The approximation is applied only to the displayed result. If you will reuse the value in another operation, retain more decimal places and round at the end. The guide to [converting kilometers to miles](/en/blog/converters/how-to-convert-kilometers-to-miles) explains the process and precision choices in more detail.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many miles are 5 kilometers?

Five kilometers is approximately `3.11 mi`.

### How many miles are 10 kilometers?

Ten kilometers is approximately `6.21 mi`.

### How many kilometers are 50 miles?

Fifty statute miles equals `80.4672 km`, or `80.47 km` rounded to two decimal places.

### How many miles are in a marathon?

A marathon is `42.195 km`, which is approximately `26.22 mi`. It is also commonly stated as 26 miles and 385 yards.

### Are the table values exact?

The factor `1 mi = 1.609344 km` is used to calculate them, but the displayed figures are rounded to two decimal places. They should therefore be read as approximations.
