## The quick difference between kilometers and miles

Kilometers and miles both measure distance, but they belong to different unit conventions. A kilometer (`km`) is a decimal multiple of the meter; the international statute mile (`mi`) equals `1.609344 km`.

```txt
1 mi = 1.609344 km
1 km ≈ 0.621371 mi
```

A mile is longer than a kilometer. When two routes have the same number, the one expressed in miles represents a greater distance: `10 mi` is approximately `16.09 km`, while `10 km` is about `6.21 mi`.

| Characteristic | Kilometer | Statute mile |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Symbol | `km` | `mi` |
| Main relationship | 1 km = 1,000 m | 1 mi = 1.609344 km |
| Convention | Metric system / SI | US customary and Imperial contexts |
| Common contexts | Maps, roads, sports and metric measurement | Roads, maps and sports where miles are used |

## What a kilometer is

One kilometer equals `1,000 meters`. Because it is a decimal multiple, converting between kilometers and meters is straightforward:

```txt
2.5 km = 2,500 m
```

The correct symbol is `km`, with both letters lowercase and no period unless it ends a sentence. It is useful for trips that would be awkward to state in meters, such as the distance between cities, a car journey or a 5K race.

A kilometer does not define the context by itself. It can describe distance traveled, route length or separation between two points. When it appears as `km/h`, it represents speed rather than distance.

## What a mile means

On roads and in everyday use, “mile” normally means the statute or international mile. [NIST](https://www.nist.gov/pml/special-publication-811/nist-guide-si-appendix-b-conversion-factors/nist-guide-si-appendix-b9) gives its equivalent as:

```txt
1 mi = 1,609.344 m = 1.609344 km
```

The symbol `mi` prevents confusion with other units. It should not be replaced with `m`, which is the symbol for a meter.

There is also a nautical mile, used in maritime and air navigation. It is not the same length: one nautical mile equals `1.852 km`. The [kilometers-to-miles calculator](/en/converters/kilometers-to-miles) works with statute miles (`mi`), not nautical miles.

## Where each unit appears

The unit depends on the country, activity and device settings. No single rule applies to every map, vehicle or event.

In the United States, the [Manual on Uniform Traffic Control Devices](https://mutcd.fhwa.dot.gov/pdfs/11th_Edition/Chapter2d.pdf) specifies how road distance signs display distances to destinations. In England, Scotland and Wales, the [Highway Code](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/the-highway-code/other-information) publishes mile-to-kilometer equivalents for interpreting road distances.

You may also see differences in situations such as:

- A map or navigation app can show kilometers or miles according to its settings.
- A car odometer may use the unit for its target market or allow the unit to be changed.
- A race may be advertised as a 5K, 10K, one-mile event or a combination of distances.
- A technical document may retain the source unit and add a conversion in parentheses.

Always check the symbol. A number by itself does not tell you whether a distance is in `km` or `mi`.

## Kilometers, miles and speed are not the same

The symbols `km` and `mi` express length or distance. Adding a unit of time changes the quantity:

| Symbol | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `km` | distance in kilometers |
| `mi` | distance in statute miles |
| `km/h` | kilometers traveled per hour |
| `mph` | miles traveled per hour |

For example, `50 mi` is a distance and `50 mph` is a speed. Although both use miles, they answer different questions and are not interchangeable.

The same applies to running: a distance may be `5 km`, while pace is stated in minutes per kilometer. Converting the distance does not automatically convert the pace or total time.

## Everyday conversion examples

These references show the difference in scale:

| Original value | Approximate equivalent |
| ---: | ---: |
| 1 km | 0.62 mi |
| 1 mi | 1.61 km |
| 5 km | 3.11 mi |
| 5 mi | 8.05 km |
| 100 km | 62.14 mi |
| 100 mi | 160.93 km |

To convert kilometers to miles:

```txt
miles = kilometers / 1.609344
```

To convert miles to kilometers:

```txt
kilometers = miles × 1.609344
```

## Which unit should you use?

The clearest option is usually to retain the unit used by the context and add a conversion when it helps the reader:

- On a sign or route, identify the original unit first.
- When traveling, set the map to the unit you can interpret quickly.
- In a technical document, follow the unit required by the project or source.
- For a race, retain the event's official distance and convert only as a reference.
- When sharing a result with people who use another system, show both units.

Do not change a number without including its symbol. Writing “20 remaining” could mean 20 kilometers or 20 miles, a difference of more than 12 kilometers.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which is longer, a kilometer or a mile?

A statute mile is longer. It equals `1.609344 km`; one kilometer is approximately `0.621371 mi`.

### Why do some countries use miles?

Measurement systems and road standards developed differently. Some contexts therefore retain miles while others use kilometers. For a specific route, follow local signs and rules.

### Does a mile always mean a statute mile?

No. On roads it normally means a statute mile, but maritime and air navigation use nautical miles. The context and symbol distinguish them.

### Can kilometers and miles also measure speed?

By themselves they measure distance. Speeds are expressed as kilometers per hour (`km/h`) or miles per hour (`mph`).

### How do I convert 100 kilometers to miles?

Divide `100` by `1.609344`. The result is `62.137119... mi`, or `62.14 mi` rounded to two decimal places.
