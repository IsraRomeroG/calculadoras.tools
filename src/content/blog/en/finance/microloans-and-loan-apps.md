A small, short-term loan can still be extremely expensive. Fixed fees consume a larger share of a small principal, and a missed seven-, fourteen- or thirty-day deadline can trigger renewals and late charges.

For lending apps, cost is only part of the review. Verify that the institution exists, that its legal details match, and that it is not demanding an advance payment to “release” a loan.

## Check the complete price

Look for the interest rate, Mexico’s CAT disclosure, origination and administration fees, insurance, late charges, payment frequency and total repayment. A `$500` fee on a `$5,000` loan already equals 10% of the amount borrowed.

Beware of daily rates. A figure that looks small per day can translate into a very high annual cost. See [annual, monthly and daily loan rates](/en/blog/finance/annual-monthly-daily-loan-rates).

## Worked microloan example

Assume:

- Principal: `$5,000`
- Annual rate used for the model: `96%`
- Term: `6 months`
- Initial fee: `$500`

The estimated payment is `$1,081.58`, with `$1,489.46` in interest. Including the fee:

```txt
6489.46 + 500 = 6989.46
```

You receive `$5,000` and repay approximately `$6,989.46`. The short term and large relative fee produce a very high annualized cost.

## Warning signs

- CAT or total repayment is not disclosed clearly.
- Fees are large relative to the principal.
- Late charges and renewal terms are vague.
- The company pressures you to accept immediately.
- It requests money upfront to activate or release credit.
- The legal entity does not appear in Mexico’s SIPRES registry.
- The app imitates the name or branding of a real institution.

SIPRES registration confirms institutional details; it does not certify that a product is affordable. Review permissions requested by the app and use only official channels.

Before accepting, enter every known charge in the [loan calculator](/en/finance/loan-calculator) and compare cash received with total cash repaid. If the offer does not let you answer those questions, walk away.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why can a microloan have such a high CAT?

High rates and fixed costs are annualized over a small principal and short term.

### Can a low daily rate still be expensive?

Yes. Convert it to a comparable period and include every fee.

### What should I verify in a lending app?

The legal name, SIPRES registration, rate, CAT, fees, contract, app permissions and official contact channels.
