In Mexico, a *crédito de nómina* is linked to the account into which a salary is deposited and is often repaid automatically from payroll funds. A personal loan need not be tied to that account.

Automatic collection does not guarantee a lower price. Compare the rate, official CAT, fees, insurance, total repayment and effect on monthly liquidity.

## Side-by-side overview

| Feature | Payroll loan | Personal loan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Repayment | May be deducted from salary deposits | Debit, transfer or other scheduled payment |
| Typical relationship | Payroll account and regular income | Depends on lender and underwriting |
| Main cash-flow issue | Deduction occurs before other spending choices | Payment must be actively budgeted |
| Cost comparison | Rate, CAT, fees and total repayment | Rate, CAT, fees and total repayment |

A payroll loan’s automatic deduction can be convenient, but it also reduces available income immediately. Ask what happens if you change employers or move your payroll account.

## Hypothetical comparison

For `$50,000` over 24 months:

| Option | Annual rate | Fee | Estimated payment | Interest | Total cost |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Payroll loan model | 18% | $0 | $2,496.21 | $9,908.92 | $59,908.92 |
| Personal loan model | 30% | $1,000 | $2,795.64 | $17,095.38 | $68,095.38 |

The payroll option is cheaper in this example only. Actual offers can reverse the result.

## Conditions to review

- Automatic deduction and its effect on cash flow.
- Treatment after a job or payroll-bank change.
- Origination fees and insurance.
- Official CAT and total repayment.
- Prepayment terms.
- Any effect on payroll portability.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does a payroll loan always have a lower rate?

No. Pricing depends on the institution, product, income and credit profile.

### What happens if I change jobs?

The contract determines how repayment continues. Ask before signing.

### Is the payment deducted automatically?

Often, but not universally. Confirm the collection method and date.
