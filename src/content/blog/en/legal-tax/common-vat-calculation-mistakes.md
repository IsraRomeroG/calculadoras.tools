## Subtracting the rate from a gross price

VAT included in a total must be extracted by division. At 16%, use `gross / 1.16`, not `gross x 0.84`.

## Mixing net and gross prices

Label every amount. “500 plus VAT” and “500 VAT included” lead to different tax bases and totals.

## Using the wrong rate

Rates vary by country, date, product, location and legal status. A calculator cannot decide which classification applies.

## Treating 0%, exempt and out of scope as identical

All may display no VAT charged to the customer, but their reporting and input-tax consequences can differ materially.

## Rounding too early

Keep sufficient precision in line calculations and apply the legally required rounding convention at the correct stage. Multiple rounded lines may not exactly match tax calculated on one aggregate base.

## Combining several taxes

Do not assume that taxes are simply added. One charge may form part of another tax’s base. Calculate each under its own rules.
