A VAT-exclusive price is the taxable base before VAT. “500 plus 20% VAT” means VAT of 100 and a gross total of 600.

A VAT-inclusive price already contains the tax. If the total is 600 at 20%, the net price is `600 / 1.20 = 500`; VAT is 100.

The common mistake is to compare one supplier’s net quotation with another supplier’s gross quotation. Convert both to the same basis and check whether delivery, fees and other taxes are included.

Use multiplication to add VAT and division to extract it:

```txt
gross = net x (1 + rate)
net = gross / (1 + rate)
```
