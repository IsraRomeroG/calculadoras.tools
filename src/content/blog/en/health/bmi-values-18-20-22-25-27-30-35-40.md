For most adults, common BMI values map to these reference categories:

| BMI | Adult interpretation |
|---:|---|
| 18 | Underweight |
| 20 | Healthy weight |
| 22 | Healthy weight |
| 25 | Overweight |
| 27 | Overweight |
| 30 | Obesity, class 1 |
| 35 | Obesity, class 2 |
| 40 | Obesity, class 3 |

Exact decimals matter at a cutoff. A displayed BMI of `25` might come from an unrounded value of `24.96` or `25.04`, so retain at least one decimal before assigning a category.

The category does not describe body composition or diagnose disease. A muscular person may have a high BMI with relatively low body fat, while someone with low muscle mass may have a value in the healthy range but other risk factors. Waist measurement, blood pressure, laboratory results, fitness and medical history add context.

Use the [BMI calculator](/en/health/bmi-calculator) for an unrounded calculation. The adult table is not appropriate for children, teenagers or the interpretation of weight gained during pregnancy.
