TDEE estimates total daily calories. Macros describe how those calories are divided among protein, fat and carbohydrate.

First choose an energy target near maintenance, below it for a deficit or above it for a surplus. Then convert macro grams to calories:

```txt
Protein:      4 kcal per gram
Carbohydrate: 4 kcal per gram
Fat:          9 kcal per gram
```

For a `2,000 kcal` target with `120 g` protein and `65 g` fat:

```txt
Protein = 120 x 4 = 480 kcal
Fat = 65 x 9 = 585 kcal
Remaining = 2,000 - 480 - 585 = 935 kcal
Carbohydrate = 935 / 4 = 234 g approximately
```

This is only an arithmetic example. Appropriate protein and fat targets depend on body size, training, preferences and health; carbohydrate can then fill the remaining energy. Percentages are convenient, but gram-based protein planning is often easier to relate to body size.

Use the [TDEE calculator](/en/health/tdee-calculator) for energy and the [macro calculator](/en/health/macro-calculator) for the distribution. Food quality, fibre, micronutrients and adherence still matter beyond the numbers.
