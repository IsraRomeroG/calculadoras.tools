For most adults, BMI from `25.0` through `29.9` is classified as overweight. Obesity begins at `30.0`.

A BMI of `25` is therefore at the lower boundary, but calculate before rounding: `24.96` remains below `25`, even if a whole-number display shows `25`.

The category does not prove that a person has excess body fat. BMI includes muscle and cannot show fat distribution. It also does not diagnose diabetes, hypertension or any other condition. Think of the result as a prompt to review additional information rather than as a verdict.

Helpful context can include waist measurement, blood pressure, glucose and lipid results, fitness, family history and weight trend. For someone with substantial muscle, a body-composition estimate may help explain the result.

Use the [BMI calculator](/en/health/bmi-calculator) to confirm the value. If weight has changed unexpectedly or other risk factors are present, discuss the complete picture with a health professional.
