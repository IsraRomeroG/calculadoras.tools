To calculate BMI with US customary units, use weight in pounds and height in total inches:

```txt
BMI = 703 x weight (lb) / height (in)^2
```

Convert feet to inches first. Multiply the feet by `12`, then add the remaining inches. A height of `5 ft 8 in` is therefore:

```txt
(5 x 12) + 8 = 68 inches
```

For a person weighing `165 lb`:

```txt
BMI = 703 x 165 / 68^2
BMI = 116,000 / 4,624
BMI = 25.1 approximately
```

The factor `703` reconciles pounds and inches with the metric definition of BMI. It is not needed when you use kilograms and metres.

Common errors include entering `5.8` as the height, squaring only the inches after the feet, or mixing pounds with the metric formula. Use either a complete metric calculation or a complete imperial one.

The [BMI calculator](/en/health/bmi-calculator) accepts imperial units and performs the conversion automatically. BMI is a screening measure; interpret the result alongside other health information.
